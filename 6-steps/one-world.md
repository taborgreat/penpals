# One world: the site is one page, and it is the world

For the builder. A change to the site's shape. Do it after matter-fixes.md; it replaces rooms.md §7 (joining spaces). It does not touch the ink law.

## The whole picture (same block in all the docs)

Five layers, one set of primitives:
1. **cleanup-and-speed.md** (now, the builder): fix the lag, the camera, the table, candles as objects, and the wet front in the law.
   - **accounts-change.md** (the builder, after cleanup §1–6, before §7): signatures make the key; sign in by username plus password, 24 words or private key.
2. **light-waves.md** (now, in parallel, a second agent): light as a range of waves, the room, the eye. Built as standalone modules with no edits to existing files. The builder integrates them afterward (cleanup-and-speed.md §7).
3. **rooms.md** (later, another agent): your profile becomes your room; walls are sheets; writing together. Starts only after layers 1 and 2 are green and one page writes smoothly in the lit room.
   - **matter-fixes.md** (the builder, after rooms phase 4a): the heat rule in the law, drips from the candle, objects as grown matter in the light, shine from the surface.
   - **one-world.md** (the builder, after matter-fixes): the site is one page, the world; houses near the start; every control becomes something the being does.
4. **outside.md** (after rooms): the laws that make circles and swings, the ground with stones and trees to pick up, break and carve, and the point of attention: keys, mouse and the eye's zoom.
5. **world.md** (last, after outside): sound as events (the pen first), weather, and the first life. Scene only, built in testable steps.

The primitives every layer follows:
- **One law.** Acts in, facts out. Only wet ink computes; dry tiles sleep. A wall is a sheet like a page.
- **Facts and the scene are separate.** Light, the room, the senses and the beings' motion never touch the ink's facts, seals or digests. The scene is a fold of the facts for display.
- **Color is what matter refuses to drink.** Ink, paper, wood and plaster all absorb by wavelength; nothing is painted.
- **Everything is an act on the chain.** Where things are and who may write are read from the chain.
- **Only acts cross the wire.** Everyone regrows the same result from the same acts in the same order.
- **Every act settles.** Its reactions run to completion under the law whether or not anyone is looking (the ink beads, sinks and dries; the candle burns down). Watching only decides what is shown.
- **One being, its senses on its head.** The person looking is one being: a body that moves and a head that turns, with the eye and the ear on the head. Moving the mouse turns the head the same way moving the pen lays ink: an act changing matter. Recorded coarsely, never every tiny motion.
- **One point of attention.** You can only be focused on one thing at a time. The keys place the body; the mouse moves whatever your attention is on (the head, the hand, the pen, the swing); the scroll sends the eye closer. No menus: only direct action.
- **Same rules everywhere.** Matter differs only by its properties, what surrounds it, and the key it was grown from. No phenomenon gets its own system.
- **Everything falls until it rests,** unless it is held, flying, or lighter than what surrounds it. Lighter rises, heavier sinks: warm smoke rises, a drip falls.
- **One rule for heat.** Matter holds heat, passes it to its neighbors, gains it from absorbed light and from burning, and loses it to the air. When a matter's heat crosses its thresholds it changes phase: wax melts and hardens, water evaporates and freezes.
- **Fire is a release loop.** Heat breaks bonds, releasing heat, light and smoke, until the fuel runs out. A candle flame is one.
- **Each sense pulls its own range.** The eye: exactly what is in view, at the detail it can see. The ear: a range around, out to where sounds fall below hearing, muffled by walls. Light: the whole room, but only at patch level. Nothing outside every sense's range is sent or drawn.

## The ruling

The whole site is one page: the 3D world. Everything else goes. You move with WASD and the mouse, Space and Shift, E to interact or change, and scroll for the eye's zoom (outside.md Part 3). There are no menus, no text forms and no separate pages for the things the world now does.

There is no mechanism for joining rooms. Houses stand near each other in one world, and people walk to each other's houses.

## Spawned matter (a shortcut for this site only)

When a new person registers, one act drops **a house with a table** for them somewhere close to the starting area.
- **The act is a fact** on the chain like any other, signed by the new key: "house placed here".
- **Placement is simple and deterministic:** the next free plot around the starting area, counted from the house acts in chain order. Everyone regrows the same houses in the same places.
- **The house is their space.** Its walls, floor, table and everything inside it are grown from the owner's key (rooms.md §1, §2). The door is a real door (outside.md §2.2: closed, it is a wall).
- **Moving closer:** the owner can move their house to another free plot with one move act, like moving a table. If two friends want to live side by side, one of them moves.
- This is matter that spawns. In full TreeOS a house would be made of marks: built by hand from what the world grows. Here it just appears, the same way the ink jar is unlimited (outside.md §2.4).

## The world

- **The outside is one shared space,** grown from the world's public seed, the way the lobby is. The houses stand in it.
- **Each house is its owner's space** inside it, grown from their key, so no two look alike.
- **Visiting is walking:** walk to someone's house and through their door. Who can enter follows the existing profile privacy rule (rooms.md §4); there is no block.
- **Writing on someone's sheets** is still possession: the sheet on your table, or your seal pressed into warm wax on theirs (rooms.md §5).
- Only what the senses reach loads (the primitives): a house with its door and windows closed loads only its outside.

## The panel: one box for what is in hand

The world has one panel: an empty, traditional GUI box that opens and closes by context. It holds whatever form fits what you hold or what is in front of you, and every change in it is an act on that matter's facts. It is only a visual render of those facts.
- **It follows the hand.** Pick up a pen and its panel opens; set it down and the panel goes away; pick up another and its panel opens.
- **Pen in hand:** its settings (nib, flex, ink flow, and the rest of what the write page's toolbar held), each a fact of that pen.
- **Paper in hand:** that sheet's settings.
- **Stamper in hand:** type the name whose signature the face takes.
- **Candle in hand:** light or put out. Melting is physical: you wait for it. Pouring is picking it up and tilting (E).
- **At a table with nothing in hand:** new paper, choosing its type, which brings a fresh sheet onto the table.
- **Not signed in:** sign in (username and password, 24 words, or private key) or register.
- Nothing else on the site keeps a screen of its own.

## The key corner, mail, and what does not exist

- **The key corner:** one corner of your own house has a thing you walk to. With it in front of you, the panel offers: change the site password (the backup that unlocks your key; your real key is the private key you saved), view your public and private key, and sign out everywhere.
- **Mailbox and letters are part of the world:** a mailbox is matter at each house, a letter is a sheet. No inbox page.
  - **Delivering:** carry your paper to someone's mailbox and click the mailbox with it in hand. That act puts the sheet in: from then on it is a fact that they have a letter.
  - **Checking the mailbox:** the owner walks to their mailbox and clicks it to take out one sheet, the oldest delivered. Taking it is the act that unpacks its facts: the sheet is now in their hand with all its ink.
  - One sheet per click; the rest wait in delivery order.
- **They do not exist:** friends, block, theme. There is no theme: the whole browser window is the world, a fullscreen 3D faxel render.
